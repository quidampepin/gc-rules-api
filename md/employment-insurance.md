# Employment Insurance - Regular Benefits

Temporary financial assistance to workers who have lost their job through no fault of their own while they look for work or upgrade their skills.

- **Administered by:** Employment and Social Development Canada
- **Effective date:** 2024-01-01
- **Status:** active

## Eligibility

You must have lost your job through no fault of your own, worked enough insurable hours, and be ready and willing to work.

- Were employed in insurable employment
- Lost your job through no fault of your own (e.g., shortage of work, seasonal layoff, mass layoff)
- Without work and without pay for at least 7 consecutive days in the last 52 weeks
- Worked the required number of insurable hours (420 to 700, depending on regional unemployment rate)
- Ready, willing, and capable of working each day
- Actively looking for work (must keep a written record of employers contacted)

## Amounts

The basic rate is 55% of your average insurable weekly earnings, up to a maximum.

### Key values

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Basic benefit rate | 55% |
| Maximum yearly insurable earnings | $63,200 |
| Maximum weekly benefit | $668 |
| Waiting period | 1 weeks |
| Minimum weeks of benefits | 14 weeks |
| Maximum weeks of benefits | 45 weeks |
| Minimum insurable hours required (varies by region, this is the lowest) | 420 hours |
| Maximum insurable hours required (highest unemployment regions) | 700 hours |
| Minimum consecutive days without work | 7 days |
| Qualifying period (look-back) | 52 weeks |

### Calculation rules

Weekly benefit = min(55% × average insurable weekly earnings, 668).

**Formula:** min(55 / 100 * average_insurable_weekly_earnings, 668)

**Duration:** Number of weeks of benefits depends on insurable hours worked and regional unemployment rate. Ranges from 14 to 45 weeks.

### Examples

- **Worker earning $52,000/year ($1,000/week), laid off** → $550/week
- **Worker earning $80,000/year, laid off** → $668/week

## How to apply

- **Online:** Apply online through the Service Canada website

## Processing time

Standard processing time is within 28 days of filing. Payments are issued after bi-weekly reports are processed.

## Fees

There are no fees to apply for EI benefits.
