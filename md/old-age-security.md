# Old Age Security

A monthly payment for people aged 65 and older who meet Canadian legal status and residence requirements.

- **Administered by:** Employment and Social Development Canada
- **Effective date:** 2024-01-01
- **Status:** active

## Eligibility

You must be 65 or older, a Canadian citizen or legal resident, and have lived in Canada for at least 10 years after age 18.

- 65 years of age or older
- Canadian citizen or legal resident at the time the OAS pension is approved
- Have lived in Canada for at least 10 years since age 18 (for pension in Canada)

## Amounts

The amount depends on how long you lived in Canada after age 18, your age, and your income.

### Key values

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum eligible age | 65 years |
| Age threshold for 10% increase | 75 years |
| Benefit increase for 75+ | 10% |
| Maximum monthly OAS pension (age 65-74) | $713.34 |
| Maximum monthly OAS pension (age 75+) | $784.67 |
| Years of residence for full pension | 40 years |
| Max years for partial pension | 39 years |
| Minimum years of residence for any pension | 10 years |
| Recovery tax (clawback) income threshold | $86,912 |
| Recovery tax rate | 15% |
| Increase per month of deferral (after age 65) | 0.6% |
| Maximum deferral period | 60 months |
| Maximum deferral increase (at age 70) | 36% |

### Calculation rules

1. Determine base pension by age bracket. 2. Prorate if less than 40 years of residence. 3. Apply clawback if income exceeds 86,912.

**Step 1 Base Pension:** Maximum monthly pension by age group

**Optional Deferral:** You can defer OAS up to 60 months past age 65. Each month increases your pension by 0.60%, up to 36% at age 70.

### Examples

- **Person aged 67, lived in Canada 40 years, income $50,000** → $713.34/month
- **Person aged 68, lived in Canada 25 years, income $40,000** → $445.84/month

## How to apply

- **Online:** Apply through My Service Canada Account
- **Mail:** Complete and mail Form ISP-3550 (Application for the Old Age Security Pension)

## Processing time

Apply 6 months before you want your pension to begin. Some applicants may be automatically enrolled.

## Fees

There is no fee to apply for or receive Old Age Security.
